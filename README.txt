Desc: This web app is to help users see all the events happening at all parks in their city. I created this app due to a problem I faced when I was younger. Whenever I wanted to go to the basketball, the towns moms would practice their zoomba dancing. Nothing against people exercising but they would always dance on the basketball court which meant I couldn't play there. If I had access to this web app. Then assuming the moms created an event at the park where they were practicing their dance I would be able to see, "Oh they are practicing today, I will save my time and not go".
